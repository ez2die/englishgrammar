## Prompt

Create a true 9:16 portrait empty-state cover using the exact approved night-sky world and star-sprite identity. Preserve the deep-indigo to muted-violet sky, restrained edge haze, rounded hills and tiny observatory. Place the full guide mascot small in the lower-left foreground, holding its brass telescope and pointing upward; place the observatory in the lower-right distance. Keep all authored elements within the lower 30% and preserve the upper 65% as a dark uncluttered safe zone for runtime title, explanation and button. Match the premium storybook game-UI style. No embedded text, letters, logos, UI, achievement badges, fixed constellation nodes or lines, large stars, moon, planets, comets, confetti, banners, extra characters, frame or border.
